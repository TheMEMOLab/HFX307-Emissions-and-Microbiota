# HFX307 – Emissions and Microbiota

## A path to sustainable animal production

Welcome to the teaching resources for **HFX307 – Emissions and Microbiota: a path to sustainable animal production**, offered at the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU).

The course explores the connections between **ruminant nutrition, microbial fermentation, methane emissions, and the rumen microbiome**, with particular emphasis on the use of molecular and bioinformatic approaches to understand microbial communities and their functions.
