## Comparison of Prokka and dbCAN
Prokka provides broad genome annotations, while dbCAN specifically identifies CAZyme families. Several xylan-, arabinan-, pectin-, starch- and cellulose-related functions appear in both datasets.

## Biological interpretation
Direct evidence: dbCAN identified numerous GH families, with GH and GT dominating. Prokka annotations include xylanases, arabinofuranosidases, beta-glucosidases, amylases, pectinases and Sus-like carbohydrate utilization proteins. Supported interpretation: the genome encodes a diverse carbohydrate-processing repertoire, particularly for plant cell-wall associated polysaccharides. Hypotheses: ecological adaptation to complex carbohydrate-rich environments requires validation.

## Limitations and uncertainty
Counts are based on provided files only. Family-to-substrate assignments are conservative and inference-based.

## Conclusions
The genome contains a large and diverse CAZyme repertoire dominated by GH families, with evidence for utilization of xylan, pectin, starch, cellulose-related and other complex carbohydrates.

## Methods
Feature counts were derived from Prokka TSV annotations. CAZy classes and families were parsed from dbCAN HMMER assignments.
