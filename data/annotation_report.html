Interpretation

Genome contains many hypothetical proteins, limiting confidence. dbCAN identifies numerous GH families and additional GT, CE, PL and CBM families, suggesting substantial carbohydrate-active enzyme potential. Evidence is strongest for xylan/arabinoxylan, starch/glycogen, pectin-related and cellulose/beta-glucan associated activities because multiple CAZy families support those categories. Assignments are family-level and not proof of substrate utilization.

Limitations

Genome annotation describes metabolic potential, not demonstrated biological activity.
